Frequently Asked Questions

What is GymLead AI?
GymLead AI is an AI-powered lead management system designed specifically for gyms. It centralizes all your enquiries and uses artificial intelligence to help you close deals faster.
How does AI Lead Scoring work?
The system analyzes available lead information (such as budget, goals, engagement level) and calculates a score out of 100, helping you prioritize leads based on their true potential to buy a membership.
Can I use GymLead AI for multiple gym branches?
Yes, depending on the selected plan (Enterprise) and setup, you can manage leads across multiple branches from a unified dashboard.
Do I need technical knowledge?
No. GymLead AI is designed to be highly intuitive and simple for gym owners, managers, and sales staff to use without any coding or technical background.
